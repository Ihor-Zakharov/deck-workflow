Principles
  1. 01One poster idea per page. Heading readable from the back row.
  2. 02Roman states, italic accents. Gold is always Fraunces italic.
  3. 03Diagrams draw themselves. SVG lines draw in, vertices pop, labels last.
  4. 04Journal chrome. "§ n" left, "Fig. n · Eq. (n)" right, NN / 20 in the footer.
  5. 05Stamps as verdicts. QED ∎ · Renorm. · 137.036 · 0.13 ppt.
  6. 06Silence is allowed. Graph paper is the ornament.
