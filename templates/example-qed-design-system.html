03 · Principles

Six rules of the notebook

01

One poster idea per page

The heading reads from the back row; everything else is commentary in Plex Sans 300.

02

Roman states, italic accents

The gold accent word is always Fraunces italic. Never gold roman, never a second accent colour.

03

Diagrams draw themselves

Every Feynman diagram is SVG with stroke-dashoffset; lines draw in, vertices pop, labels fade last.

04

Journal chrome

Head: "§ n · title"; right: "Fig. n · Eq. (n)". Footer: deck title · NN / 20. Mono, uppercase, 0.18em.

05

Stamps as verdicts

Circular stamps carry the slide's verdict: QED ∎, Renorm., 137.036, 0.13 ppt, Shut up and calculate.

06

Silence is allowed

Grid paper is the ornament. At least three slides carry almost nothing; the loud ones need them.
