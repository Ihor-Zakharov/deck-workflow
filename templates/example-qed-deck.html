§ 1 · What QED isFig. 1 · Two fields, one vertex
Definition

Light, matter,
nothing else

QED is the relativistic quantum field theory of charged particles and photons. It has one interaction and one free number, α. From it follow all of chemistry, optics, lasers, antennas, and the colour of gold.

It is the first theory that was both quantum and Lorentz-invariant and gave finite answers. Every later quantum field theory is built on its template.

Matter

ψ · the Dirac field

Spin-½ electrons and positrons as excitations of one field. Antimatter appears automatically from relativity: the equation has negative-energy solutions.

Light

Aμ · the Maxwell field

Photons as quanta of the electromagnetic potential. Classical waves are coherent states of very many of them; your antenna emits 10²⁴ per second.

Interaction

−e ψ̄γμψ Aμ · the vertex

One electron absorbs or emits one photon. Every process in QED is a chain of these elementary events, each weighted by √α.

§ 2 · Chronology1927–1965
Thirty-eight years from the first quantized field to the prize

Four people, three formalisms,
one theory

1927 · 1928

Dirac

Quantizes the radiation field, then writes the relativistic electron equation. Spin and antimatter fall out; so do infinities.

1930s

The infinities

Oppenheimer, Weisskopf: the electron's self-energy diverges. The theory works at first order and breaks at second.

1947

Lamb shift

Lamb and Retherford measure a 1058 MHz split Dirac forbids. Shelter Island; Bethe computes it on the train home.

1946 · 1948

Tomonaga · Schwinger

Covariant renormalization. Schwinger's α/2π correction to the electron's magnetic moment matches Kusch and Foley.

1948 · 1949

Feynman · Dyson

Diagrams booed at Pocono; Dyson proves all three approaches are the same theory and that it is renormalizable to all orders.

1965

Nobel Prize

Tomonaga, Schwinger, Feynman "for their fundamental work in quantum electrodynamics". Dyson is not on the list.

Demand that the phase of ψ may be chosen independently at every point: ψ → eiα(x)ψ. The free Dirac term alone is not invariant.

§ 4 · The coupling constantEq. (3)
The number on every theorist's wall
α = 1/137.035 999 177(21)

The strength of the vertex, with every unit cancelled out. It is the same on Earth, in quasars, and in every system of units. Nobody knows why it has this value.

"It has been a mystery ever since it was discovered more than fifty years ago, and all good theoretical physicists put this number up on their wall and worry about it."

Richard Feynman, QED: The Strange Theory of Light and Matter, 1985

Because α is small, each extra photon exchanged costs a factor of ~1/137 in amplitude. That is what makes perturbation theory work, and QED computable.

§ 5 · Feynman rulesFig. 2 · Momentum space
A grammar with three symbols

Draw the picture, read off the integral

Line

Electron propagator

A straight line with an arrow. Backward in time it is a positron.

Wave

Photon propagator

A wavy line. The 1/q² is Coulomb's law in disguise.

Vertex

Vertex

Charge, energy and momentum conserved at every dot. Each vertex brings a factor of e, i.e. √α in the amplitude.

Then: integrate over every internal momentum ∫d⁴k/(2π)⁴, multiply by −1 for each closed fermion loop, sum all diagrams, square the total. That is the whole of QED calculus.

Four pictures that cover most of electrodynamics

Fig. 3

Møller scattering

Two electrons repel by trading one virtual photon. In the static limit the amplitude is exactly the Coulomb potential.

A photon is absorbed, the electron propagates off-shell, a new photon is emitted. Klein–Nishina, 1929: the first QED cross section.

Electron meets positron, two photons leave. Read sideways it is pair creation; read across, Bhabha scattering. One drawing, three processes.

A photon briefly becomes an electron–positron pair. The vacuum screens charge: this loop makes α grow with energy.

§ 5 · Sum over historiesEq. (4) · Table 1
Feynman's rule of the game

Every way it could happen, added up

Amplitudes, not probabilities, are what add. Interference between histories is the whole content of quantum mechanics; the diagrams are just a bookkeeping of which histories exist and what each one weighs.

Each extra loop brings a factor of α/π ≈ 0.0023, and roughly ten times more diagrams to compute.

§ 6 · Infinities and renormalizationEq. (5) · Fig. 7
The trick that turned nonsense into twelve digits

The bare electron is never observed

Problem

Self-energy diverges

An electron keeps emitting and reabsorbing its own photons. Integrated to infinite momentum, the loop is logarithmically infinite.

Insight

Mass and charge are measured, not predicted

Cut off at Λ. Write the bare mass as m₀ = m − δm(Λ). Express every prediction through the physical m and e that experiments see. All Λ-dependence cancels — Dyson 1949, to every order.

Only two counterterms are ever needed. That is what "renormalizable" means, and why QED predicts anything at all.

Consequence

Charge runs

Vacuum polarization screens the charge. Look closer and it grows: 1/137 at atomic scales, 1/128.9 at the Z boson mass. Measured at LEP, exactly as predicted.

Dissent · Dirac 1975

"Sensible mathematics involves neglecting a quantity when it is small — not neglecting it just because it is infinitely great and you do not want it!" Wilson's answer (1971): renormalization is not a trick but the physics of scale.

§ 8 · QED for radiophysicistsTable 3 · Where the photon hides
Your transmitter is a QED experiment

Where is the photon
in your antenna?

1.5 × 10²⁴ / s

Photons from 1 W at 1 GHz

A coherent state with that many quanta is indistinguishable from a Maxwell wave. Radio is classical because hν is tiny, not because QED switches off.

hν = kT

6.2 THz at 300 K · 0.42 GHz at 20 mK

Above this line photons are countable and shot noise wins; below it, thermal noise. This is why a 5 GHz transmon lives in a dilution refrigerator.

−206 dBm/Hz

Vacuum noise hν/2 at 10 GHz

Thirty decibels under Johnson noise at room temperature (−174 dBm/Hz). A phase-preserving amplifier must add at least this: the quantum limit of every receiver.

Spontaneous emission is stimulated emission driven by vacuum fluctuations. Every LNA, mixer and radiometer sits on top of that zero-point field.

§ 8 · QED in the laboratory todayFig. 8–13
Six places where the vacuum does work

Not history: instrumentation

01

Circuit QED

Transmon qubits are artificial atoms coupled to microwave resonators; readout uses Josephson parametric amplifiers at the quantum limit. QED at 5 GHz.

02

Cavity QED

Single atoms and single photons in superconducting cavities; Haroche and Wineland, Nobel 2012. Rabi oscillations of one quantum, watched live.

03

Casimir force

Two plates attract because they exclude vacuum modes. Lamoreaux 1997, agreement to 5%; now a design constraint in MEMS.

04

Squeezed light

LIGO injects squeezed vacuum to beat shot noise by ~3 dB. Since 2023, frequency-dependent squeezing across the whole band.

05

Muonic hydrogen

A muon orbits 200× closer; the Lamb shift then measures the proton's radius: 0.84 fm (Pohl 2010), the "proton radius puzzle".

06

Strong-field QED

Schwinger limit 1.3×10¹⁸ V/m: the vacuum sparks into pairs. Petawatt lasers (ELI) approach it via boosted electron beams.

§ 8 · Where the theory endsOpen problems
Honest accounting

Perfect, and incomplete

QED's precision is not evidence that it is final. It is the low-energy corner of a larger theory, and its own mathematics tells us so.

  1. 1952Dyson's argument. The perturbation series has zero radius of convergence: it is asymptotic. Twelve digits come from an expansion that ultimately diverges.
  2. 1955Landau pole. The running coupling blows up near 10²⁸⁶ eV. Harmless in practice, fatal in principle: pure QED cannot be complete.
  3. 1967Electroweak unification. Glashow–Weinberg–Salam absorb QED into SU(2)×U(1); the photon is a mixture of W³ and B. QED is a broken-symmetry remnant.
  4. 2020α from Cs vs Rb. Two atom-interferometer measurements disagree by 5.5σ. Until resolved, the electron g−2 test is limited by α, not by QED.
  5. 2025Hadrons in the loop. Muon g−2 agrees with lattice QCD but not with e⁺e⁻ data-driven estimates. The tension moved from QED to the data.

ConclusionsQuod erat demonstrandum
Summary

Four things to take home

01

Symmetry writes the theory. Ask for a local phase freedom and the photon, its coupling, and Maxwell's equations are forced.

02

Diagrams are integrals. Feynman's pictures are a grammar for perturbation theory, and α ≈ 1/137 is why the grammar converges in practice.

03

Renormalization is physics, not surgery. The bare electron is unobservable; charge and mass depend on the scale you look at.

04

Radio is QED with 10²⁴ photons. The vacuum sets your noise floor, your amplifier's limit, and the temperature of your quantum computer.
